# Printed-card typos — Fifth Batch (Set 5)

Every typo **printed on the card images** in this set, shown **inside the full sentence** it appears in, with the corrected sentence so the art can be recreated (e.g. in Card Conjurer) without the mistake. Verified by reading each card image directly. Only custom (AI-art) cards are listed — real Scryfall cards print correctly. Intentional pun names, flavor stylization, and custom keywords are not treated as typos.

**18 correction(s) across 16 card(s).**

## Armix of the Scrapped Past

- **Printed:** “During your turn, you may activate activated abilities of artifact and creature cards in your graveyard as though as they were on the battlefield.”
  **Fix:** “During your turn, you may activate activated abilities of artifact and creature cards in your graveyard as though they were on the battlefield.”  — _removed doubled 'as' ("as though as they were" -> "as though they were")_

## Armored Regent

- **Printed:** “Requiem — For as long as this creature is tapped, whenever on or more cards enters or leaves your graveyard, put a +1/+1 counter on target creature you control.”
  **Fix:** “Requiem — For as long as this creature is tapped, whenever one or more cards enter or leave your graveyard, put a +1/+1 counter on target creature you control.”  — _"on" changed to "one"_

## Confuse

## Fear of Technology
